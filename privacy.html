Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy for Lula Discord Bot

Last updated: October 2, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Lula ("Lula", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the Lula Discord bot or visit the Lula website.

Lula is a Discord bot designed to provide XP and leveling, economy, moderation, tickets, statistics, and other community features.

2. Information We Collect

Lula may process limited information available through Discord that is necessary to provide its features.

Discord Account Information

  • Discord user ID
  • Discord server (guild) ID
  • Information required to display users or perform requested commands

XP and Level Information

  • XP earned through server activity
  • Level information derived from XP
  • XP progress information used by the leveling system
  • Level-related server roles

Economy Information

  • LH Coins balance
  • Economy activity required for commands such as daily, work, and pay

Moderation Information

  • Warnings associated with Discord users
  • Information necessary to perform moderation commands

Server Configuration

  • Discord server IDs
  • Bot configuration required for server features
  • Information required to operate tickets and other configured features

Message Content

Lula may process message content when necessary to determine whether a message qualifies for the XP system or to respond to bot functionality.

Lula does not intentionally use message content to build advertising profiles or sell message content to third parties.

3. How We Use Information

Information processed by Lula is used only to provide, maintain, and improve the bot's functionality.

  • Providing XP and leveling features
  • Assigning and maintaining level roles
  • Providing economy features
  • Displaying leaderboards and rankings
  • Providing moderation features
  • Managing tickets
  • Maintaining bot configuration
  • Providing requested Discord commands
  • Preventing abuse and maintaining service security

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information or Discord user data.

Lula uses Discord's APIs and services to provide its functionality. Information processed through Discord is subject to Discord's own policies and terms.

We may disclose information when required by applicable law or when necessary to protect the security and integrity of Lula.

5. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the relevant Lula features or for legitimate operational, security, or legal purposes.

When information is no longer required for the operation of Lula, it may be deleted or otherwise removed from our active systems, subject to applicable legal requirements.

6. Data Deletion

Users may request deletion of data associated with their use of Lula.

To request deletion, contact us using the contact information below and include your Discord user ID and enough information for us to identify the relevant data.

Contact: YOUR_EMAIL@example.com

We may need to verify the request before deleting data. Some information may be retained where required by law or where reasonably necessary for security or legal purposes.

7. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed by Lula against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

8. Children's Privacy

Lula is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from individuals who are not permitted to use Discord or its services under applicable age requirements.

Lula relies on Discord's age and platform requirements.

9. Third-Party Services

Lula operates through Discord and uses Discord's APIs. Discord is a third-party platform and its own terms and privacy policies also apply to your use of Discord.

You can review Discord's Privacy Policy on the Discord website .

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Lula's functionality, data practices, or applicable legal requirements change.

The latest version will be published on this page.
